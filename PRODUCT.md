# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Front-end and Android engineers debugging Android WebView pages in offline, USB, or intranet environments.

## Product Purpose

ztools discovers debuggable Android WebViews, collects runtime evidence through ADB and CDP, and turns noisy page, network, console, replay, and logcat signals into a focused diagnosis that can be shared.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, technical.

## Anti-references

Do not become a decorative DevTools clone, a marketing dashboard, or an AI chat surface that hides the raw evidence.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the most likely root cause and keep the evidence one click away.
- Reuse Chrome DevTools for deep inspection instead of duplicating it.
- Preserve offline workflows and avoid external services by default.
- Prefer dense, aligned task panels over oversized presentation UI.
- Make exported reports useful to another engineer without extra context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear contrast, keyboard-reachable controls, stable layouts, and no required motion. Debugging often happens under time pressure, so states and actions must be explicit.
